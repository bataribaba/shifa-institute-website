About us

Our mission

Based in Michigan, The Shifa Institute is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization that aims to provide Muslim Americans and other minorities with education, references, and resources for mental health. We aim to take a multifaceted approach, including supporting research and a psychological perspective regarding mental health, while also maintaining a spiritual identity and philosophy. Our goal is to encourage the conversation of mental health within our community while breaking down the stigmas that many hold against it.

The Shifa Institute does not discriminate against any beliefs, customs, or values, regardless of race, culture, gender, religion, ethnicity, or orientation. Everyone is welcome.

Our history

In 2019, Salman Pervez, a master's student in clinical psychology, founded Spartan Shifa at Michigan State University to create a support network for students interested in Muslim mental health.

In 2024, Azaan Jamil, a premedical psychology undergraduate, established Dearborn Shifa at the University of Michigan-Dearborn, to raise community awareness of Muslim mental health from a research and psychological perspective. The two chapters began collaborating on events and workshops.

Months later, Shayan Faraz, who has a background in business, joined Dearborn Shifa. Together, the three founded The Shifa Institute, with Spartan Shifa and Dearborn Shifa as its foundations, to provide mental health resources, wellness, and education to the Muslim American and minority community.

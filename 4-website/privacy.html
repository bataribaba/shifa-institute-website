Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Shifa Institute ("The Shifa Institute", "we", "us", or "our"), a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization based in Michigan, handles personal information in connection with this website (the "Site"). By using the Site, you agree to this Policy.

1. Summary

  • The Site has no user accounts, no sign-up forms, and no advertising.
  • We do not set our own cookies, and we do not run analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking tools on the Site.
  • Searches and filters you use on the Resources page are processed in your own browser. We do not receive or record them.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • Donations are processed by third-party payment providers. We do not receive or store your full card or bank account details.

2. We are not a health care provider

The Shifa Institute provides education and community programming. We are not a health care provider or a "covered entity" under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), and information you send us is not protected health information under that law. Please do not send us medical records, diagnoses, or other sensitive health details. If you do, we will use them only to respond to you and will not share them, except as required by law or to protect someone's safety.

3. Information we collect

Information you choose to give us. If you email us, message us on social media, or speak with us at an event, we receive the information you provide, such as your name, contact details, school or organization, and the content of your message.

Donation records. When you donate, the payment provider you choose (Donorbox, PayPal, Venmo, or Zelle through your bank) collects the details needed to process your gift under its own privacy policy. We receive a record of the donation, which may include your name, email address, mailing address if you provide one, the amount, the date, and the payment method type.

Technical information collected by our host. The company that hosts the Site automatically records standard server log data, such as IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and the date and time, in order to deliver the Site and keep it secure.

Information we do not collect. We do not collect information through cookies of our own, we do not build profiles of visitors, and we do not knowingly collect precise location data.

4. Third-party content on the Site

Some parts of the Site are provided by other companies. When your browser loads them, those companies receive technical information such as your IP address and may set their own cookies or similar technologies, under their own privacy policies:

  • Instagram feed. The home page displays our recent Instagram posts through SnapWidget.
  • Donation form. The Donate Now page displays a form hosted by Donorbox, which works with payment processors to complete donations.

Fonts, images, and all other files are served from the Site itself. When you follow a link to another website, including any organization on our Resources page, that website's own privacy policy applies.

5. How we use information

  • to respond to questions, requests, and corrections;
  • to process and acknowledge donations, send receipts, and keep the financial records a nonprofit is required to keep;
  • to organize events and work with student chapters, partners, and volunteers who contact us;
  • to maintain the security and operation of the Site; and
  • to comply with the law and protect the rights and safety of our community and others.

We do not use personal information for automated decision-making or profiling.

6. When we share information

We share personal information only in these situations:

  • Service providers that help us operate, such as website hosting, email, and payment and donation processing, who may use the information only to provide those services;
  • Legal and safety reasons, when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is necessary to protect the safety, rights, or property of any person;
  • Organizational changes, such as a merger or transfer of our programs to another nonprofit, in which case the information would remain subject to this Policy; and
  • With your consent, for example if you ask us to introduce you to a chapter or partner.

We do not publish donor names without permission.

7. How long we keep information

We keep emails and messages for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of our work. We keep donation records for as long as tax and nonprofit laws require, which is generally at least seven years. We delete or anonymize information when we no longer need it.

8. Security

The Site is built as static pages, is served over an encrypted connection, and does not store visitor data. We limit access to donation and contact records to people who need it for their work. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may ask us to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. To make a request, email theshifainstitute@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and will not treat you differently for making a request. We may need to keep certain records, such as donation records, where the law requires it.

You can block or delete cookies set by third-party content through your browser settings. Because we do not track visitors across websites, the Site does not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals.

10. Children's privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Visitors from outside the United States

The Shifa Institute operates in the United States. If you use the Site from another country, your information will be processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those where you live.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

13. Contact

Questions or requests about this Policy can be sent to theshifainstitute@gmail.com. We currently do not accept postal forms of contact.
